## Why Choose CRUSHBURG?

### Benefit 1: Strong & Scalable Business Model
A well-structured QSR format designed for quick setup, efficient operations, and scalable franchise growth across multiple locations.

### Benefit 2: Standardized Quality & Taste Consistency
Centralized SOPs, product standardization, and quality control systems ensure customers get the same great taste and experience at every outlet.

### Benefit 3: Complete Franchisor Operated System (FICO - Profit Partner Model)
A unique franchise structure where:
- The **franchisor manages** the complete operational ecosystem
- The **franchisee acts primarily as a profit partner**, reducing day-to-day operational burden
- All major operational expenses are borne by the franchisor
- Franchisee focuses on business ownership and profit participation
- Simple one-time franchise investment model with structured operational backing

## Business Models

### 1. CAFÉ Model (500 sq ft)
**Target:** High-footfall mall food courts

**Concept:** Fast-casual beverage café focusing on premium yet affordable crush drinks, shakes, coffees, and quick bites. Targets teens, families, and young professionals.

**Space Utilization:**
- Front-facing service counter with POS and digital menu boards: ~100 sq ft
- Beverage preparation area (blenders, espresso machine, worktops): ~120 sq ft
- Cold storage and refrigeration: ~60 sq ft
- Dry storage: ~50 sq ft
- Sink/cleaning zone: ~30 sq ft
- Remaining: Staff movement space
- **No in-store seating required** (mall food court provides seating)

---

### 2. CAFETERIA Model (1500 sq ft)
**Target:** High-traffic areas - malls, commercial streets, IT parks, shopping hubs

**Concept:** Premium fast-casual dining combining signature beverages with expanded food menu and comfortable seating to increase customer dwell time and average billing value.

**Space Utilization:**
- Kitchen and beverage preparation zone (separate hot and cold sections): ~400 sq ft
- Service counter and display area: ~150 sq ft
- Seating area (50–70 guests capacity - sofas, tables, bar seating): ~700 sq ft
- Storage, wash area, and staff movement: Remaining space

**Interior:** Modern décor, warm lighting, strong brand visibility, Instagram-friendly environment

---

### 3. DRIVE CAFETERIA Model (2500 sq ft)
**Target:** Prime roadside, highway-facing, or high-traffic urban locations

**Concept:** Premium large-format café integrating spacious dine-in seating with a fully functional drive-thru system, capturing both walk-in and vehicle-based customers.

**Space Utilization:**
- Fully equipped kitchen (separate hot, cold, beverage sections): ~600 sq ft
- Service counter and display area: ~200 sq ft
- Indoor seating (80–120 guests - sofas, table seating, community tables): ~1200 sq ft
- Storage, wash areas, staff zones, circulation pathways: Remaining space
- **Dedicated drive-thru lane:** Order station, payment window, pickup counter

**Service Time:** 3–7 minutes for drive-thru
